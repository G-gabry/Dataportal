The Applications are open to apply for the **UNEP Georgetown Fellowship 2025**. It is a **[Fully Funded Fellowship in the USA](https://opportunitiescorners.com/list-of-fully-funded-fellowships-in-usa/) **to earn an **LLM Degree** in Environmental and Energy Law from Georgetown University Law Center and gain a **three-month** International **Internship** Experience with the **United Nations** at **UNEP headquarters in Kenya** or any regional offices. The **Objective** of the UNEP Georgetown Fellowship is to train public sector lawyers from developing countries who work on environmental law, natural resources law, or related legal fields, to strengthen the environmental rule of law. There is **no application fee** and the fellowship covers the Cost of **Tuition** , **Stipend** , **Airfare** Tickets, **Health** Insurance, and Textbooks. The UNEP Georgetown Fellowship offers a US LLM and three 3-month internships. More details are given below.
## **Details About UNEP Georgetown Fellowship 2025 in the USA**
  * **Host Country** : USA
  * **Location** : Georgetown University, Washington D.C.
  * **Program Name** : LLM in Environmental and Energy Law
  * **Duration** : 3 Months Internship
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 5th February 2025**

**Also Check:[Edinburgh Visiting Fellowship Program 2025 in UK (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/edinburgh-visiting-fellowship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The UNEP Fellowship covers:
  * Full Tuition for Georgetown Law LL.M. (including Summer Experience Program)
  * All Required Textbooks
  * Monthly Stipend for UNEP Internship
  * Health Insurance (LL.M. & Internship)
  * Flights to/from Washington D.C. (for LL.M.)
  * Flights to/from UNEP Posting (for Internship)

**Note** : Fellows are responsible for covering living expenses in Washington, D.C., including housing, food, and other personal costs, **during the LL.M. program**.
**Also Check:[Commonwealth Startup Fellowship 2025 (Fully Funded to Ghana)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/commonwealth-startup-fellowship/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
To be eligible for this fellowship, candidates must:
  * Be residents of a developing country.
  * Hold a law degree recognized in their home country.
  * Be currently employed by a national or local government.
  * Be actively practicing environmental, energy, natural resources, or related law.

**Note** : Fellows are required to return to their home country and continue working in public sector environmental law for a minimum of three years.
**Also Check:[World Press Institute Fellowship 2025 in USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/world-press-institute-fellowship/)**
### **How to Apply for the UNEP Georgetown Fellowship 2025?**
Applicants must read the information on the application process from the official website. The Link to the official website is below.
[APPLY FOR THE UNEP GEORGETOWN FELLOWSHIP](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/admissions-aid/graduate-admissions/scholarships-fellowships/unep-georgetown-law-center-global-enviroment-sustainability-law-fellowship/)
