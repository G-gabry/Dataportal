UNNES stands for the Universitas Negeri Semarang (UNNES). It is a government-funded university located in Semarang, Central Java, Indonesia. The University was founded in 1965. The applications are open for the **UNNES NEXTSHIP Scholarship 2024**. The scholarship is available to pursue **Undergraduate** , **Master** , and **Doctoral** Degree programs available at UNNES. Securing a scholarship in Indonesia is easy. I have also created a list of **[BS MS PhD Scholarships in Indonesia](https://opportunitiescorners.com/bs-ms-phd-scholarships-in-indonesia/)**.
The UNNES NEXTSHIP Scholarship will cover tuition fees, registration fees, monthly stipends, living allowance, Institutional Development Fee, and Dorm Fee. You can apply **without IELTS/TOEFL** if you are from an English-speaking country or have completed your previous education at the English Medium Institute. Choose Indonesia for Affordable Education, High-Quality Education, and Beautiful Surroundings. More details about the UNNES NEXTSHIP Scholarship are given below.
## **Details About Indonesia UNNES NEXTSHIP Scholarship 2024**
  * **Host Country** : Indonesia
  * **Degree Level** : Bachelors, Masters, PhD
  * **Financial Benefits** : Partially Funded
  * **Deadline: 31st May 2024**

**Also Check:[UM ISS Scholarship 2024 in Indonesia (Fully Funded Scholarship)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/um-iss-scholarship-in-indonesia/)**
### **Scholarship Coverage**
The UNNES NEXTSHIP scholarship will cover:
  * Registration Fee
  * Tuition Fee
  * Institutional Development Fee
  * Dormitory Fee for Masters and PhD students
  * Living Allowance for Masters and PhD students

**Also Check:[University of Jambi Scholarship 2024 in Indonesia (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/university-of-jambi-scholarship-indonesia/)**
### **Scholarship Requirements for Undergraduates/Masters/Doctorate**
  * The maximum age to apply is 25 years old for undergrads, 35 years for masters, and 40 years for PhD students.
  * Have a high school diploma/degree.
  * Provide a Letter of Recommendation from the Indonesian Embassy, from the employer/direct supervisor, and previous schools.
  * Provide proof of citizenship such as a passport. (ID card is not acceptable)
  * Online application form.

**Note** : If you do not have any IELTS/TOEFL, you can submit an official letter from your previous home university to replace the English proficiency test sheet.
Applicants from English-speaking countries do not need English Proficiency Test results.
**Also Check:[Malta Government Endeavour Scholarship 2024-25 (Apply Now)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/malta-government-endeavour-scholarship/)**
### **How to Apply for the UNNES NEXTSHIP Scholarship?**
Read all the requirements and the guidelines. Prepare all the documents. Click on Apply now and continue the online application process. The link to the official website is given below.
[VISIT HERE TO APPLY FOR THE UNNES NEXTSHIP SCHOLARSHIP](https://unnes.ac.id/admission/en/nextship/#informasi-lengkap)
