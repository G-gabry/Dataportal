The Applications are now open for the **Russia JINR Summer Student Program 2026**. It is a fully funded Program at the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research (JINR), Russia. The Program is open to **all the Nationals** from all around the world. Participants will stay and spend their time in **Dubna** , Russia, for 8 weeks solving scientific and engineering tasks under the guidance of JINR Experts. There is **no application fee,** and IELTS is also not required. The JINR Summer Student Program is open to **Undergraduates** , **master’s** , and **PhD** Students. Air Tickets, Visas, Insurance, Accommodations, Stipends, and all other expenses are covered by the Joint Institute for Nuclear Research (JINR). Below are more details about the requirements, financial benefits, and the application process.
## **Details About the Russia JINR Summer Student Program 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Russia
  * **Location** : Dubna
  * **Program Start Date** : 28 June, 2026
  * **Duration** : 8 Weeks
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 3rd March 2026**

**Also Check:[FuSEP Summer Research Program 2026 in China (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/fusep-summer-research-program/)**
### **Objective of the JINR Summer Program**
The JINR Summer Student Program was formerly known as the START Summer Student Program. The objective of this program is to attract Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD students specializing in science, engineering, and IT. The goal is to connect JINR with the next generation of Young Minds.
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Round Airfare Travel Tickets
  * Visa Cost
  * Insurance
  * Accommodation
  * Daily Allowance of 2000 Roubles per day.

**Also Check:[CALTECH University Summer Program 2026 in USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/caltech-university-summer-research-program/)**
### **Areas of Summer Program**
  * Science
  * Engineering
  * IT

### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The Program is open to all the Nationals.
  * Applicants specializing in STEM fields (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics).
  * Applicants from their 3rd year of undergraduate studies up to the 1st year of their PhD program.
  * This program is for those who have never participated in the START program before.

**Also Check:[Bulgaria INSAIT Graduate Fellowship 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/bulgaria-insait-graduate-fellowship/)**
### **How to Apply for the Russia JINR Summer Student Program 2026?**
Applicants have to apply online from the official website. Before applying, ensure that you meet the program requirements and attach all required documents. The Link to the official website is below.
