The applications are now open for the **Future Action Conference 2026** in **Morocco**. Organized by the Organisation for Global Peace and Sustainability (OGPS), this high-level international forum brings together emerging leaders, innovators, and changemakers from across the world from **1–4 May 2026** in **Marrakesh** , Morocco. There are a total of **70 scholarships** available, divided into fully funded and Partially Funded Categories.**30 Fully funded** and **40 partially funded** slots. IELTS/TOEFL is not required. The conference provides a global platform for leadership development, meaningful dialogue, and action-oriented solutions addressing today’s most pressing global challenges. More details are below.
## **Details About Future Action Conference 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Morocco
  * **Location** : Marrakesh
  * **Duration** : 4 Days
  * **Dates** : 1–4 May 2026
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 25th March 2026**

**Also Check:[FuSEP Summer Research Program 2026 in China (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/fusep-summer-research-program/)**
### **Number of Seats**
  * **Fully Funded Scholarships** : 30

### **Financial Benefits**
The Fully Funded Scholarship Covers:
  * Round-trip international airfare
  * Conference accommodation
  * Visa support and guidance
  * Meals during the conference
  * Daily breakfast
  * Speaking opportunity at the conference
  * Official conference merchandise
  * Certificate of participation from OGPS

**Also Check:[NEXT Milan Forum 2026 (Fully Funded to Italy)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/next-milan-forum/)**
### **Conference Themes**
  * Youth Leadership & Global Citizenship
  * Peacebuilding & Conflict Resolution
  * Governance, Policy & Youth Participation
  * Sustainable Development & the UN SDGs
  * Climate Action & Environmental Responsibility
  * Social Justice, Inclusion & Human Rights
  * Innovation, Advocacy & Community Impact

### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Age between 16 and 47 years
  * Open to all nationalities worldwide
  * No English language test required
  * Open to participants from all educational and professional backgrounds
  * Students, professionals, activists, researchers, entrepreneurs, and youth leaders are all encouraged to apply.

**Also Check:[Dubai Business Associates Program 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/dubai-business-associates-program/)**
### **Application Fee**
  * £9.99 (Charged by the Hosting Organization)

### **How to Apply for the Future Action Conference 2026 Morocco?**
Complete the online application form and pay the application fee. The link to the official website is below.
[APPLY FOR THE FUTURE ACTION CONFERENCE MOROCCO](https://ogps.uk/fac2026/)
**Disclaimer** : Please note that this program requires an application fee to be considered for the scholarship category. Opportunities Corner does not charge this fee and is not responsible for any losses or damages related to the application fee.
