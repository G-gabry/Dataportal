On behalf of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Mexican Agency for International Development Cooperation (AMEXCID) invited Foreign citizens to apply for the **Fully Funded Mexico Government Scholarship 2024**. The call is open to citizens of more than **180** countries. The scholarship is awarded for full-time **Undergraduate** , **Master** , **PhD** , **Postgraduate** Research, and **Post** **Doctoral** programs. In this scholarship, more than 90 Mexican higher education institutes are participating.
The scholarship covers**all the expenses** such as Airfare, Tuition Fee, Registration, Monthly stipend, accommodation, visa costs, and health insurance. I have attached the English-translated version of the Document Guide. The Mexican Government is thankful for choosing Mexico as your educational destination! The Benefits, and Application Process of the 2024 Mexican Government Scholarship Program for International Students are given below.
## **Details About Mexico Government Scholarship 2024**
  * **Host Country:** Mexico
  * **Scholarship by:** Mexican Agency for International Development Cooperation (AMEXCID)
  * **Degree Level:** Undergraduate, Masters, Doctoral, Post Doctorate
  * **Financial Coverage:** Fully funded
  * **Deadline: 28th June 2024**

### **Eligible Countries**
Applicants from the following countries are eligible to apply. Check the **[List of eligible countries](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/916003/ANEXO_5_PAISES_ELEGIBLES.pdf)**.
### **Financial Benefits**
The holders of the scholarship will receive the following support:
  * Monthly Stipend
  * Enrollment and Tuition Fee covered
  * Medical Coverage
  * Visa Fee waiver
  * International Flight Tickets
  * In City Transportation

**Also Check:[Group of Brazilian Universities (GCUB) Scholarship 2025 (800 Scholarships)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/group-of-brazilian-universities-gcub-scholarship/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Scholarship **application form** , duly completed and signed.
  * **A letter** detailing the reasons that motivate you to carry out the studies or research in Mexico. (Maximum 2 pages, with letter Arial size 12 and single line spacing).
  * **Curriculum vitae**. (Maximum 3 pages, with Arial font size 12 and single line spacing).
  * **Letter of academic acceptance** from the Mexican institution recipient or response of the HEI to the application for admission (see Requirements section of the acceptance letter).
  * Letter of commitment to **return to the country** of origin.
  * Letter of acceptance of the terms of the Call.
  * Copy of the **birth certificate**.
  * Copy of the **valid passport** in color and open book format or government-issued **photo ID**.
  * Copy of the **medical certificate** issued by a public institution or private health, with a maximum of 3 (three) months before the presentation of the application, indicating that the Applicant is in good health.
  * In those cases in which Spanish is not the applicant’s mother tongue, must submit a copy of proof of advanced knowledge of the **Spanish language** issued by any university or educational center for its accreditation, which guarantees a minimum level of B2, within the Common Framework European Reference.
  * Letter in which the applicant states under protest to say truth, that the information provided is true.
  * All documents and forms must be **in Spanish or submitted with translations into Spanish**.

**Also Check:[Italian Government (MAECI) Scholarship 2024-25 (Apply Now)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/italian-government-maeci-scholarship/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Students having **nationality from any one of the countries** listed above are eligible to apply for a scholarship.
  * Applicants currently **not living in Mexico**.

### **How to Apply**
You have to fill out the application in Spanish. **Apply Online** at Academic Cooperation Management System (SIGCA): **[https://sigca.sre.gob.mx](https://sigca.sre.gob.mx/)**
It is advised you please use Google Translator to translate the application guidelines and other files into English. The link to the official website is given below.
