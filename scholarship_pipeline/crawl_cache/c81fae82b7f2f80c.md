The Applications are open to apply for the **One Young World Summit Roche Scholarship 2025** in **Germany**. The One Young World Summit is a **[Fully Funded International Summit](https://opportunitiescorners.com/list-of-conferences-and-youth-programs/)** of Young Leaders from all around the World. Roche UK is offering a **Fully Funded Scholarship** to the **Nationals of all countries** to attend a One Young World Summit 2025 in Munich, Germany. The OYW Summit 2025 is Taking Place from **3 – 6 November 2025**. More than **2000+ Participants** will be there from different countries and academic Fields. There is no application Fee and IELTS is **not** required. The One Young World Summit is the world’s largest and most influential gathering of young leaders. Below you will find more details on the Financial benefits, eligibility, and the application process.
## **Details About One Young World Summit Roche Scholarship 2025 in Germany**
  * **Host Country** : Germany
  * **Venue** : Munich
  * **Scholarship Name** : Roche Scholarship
  * **OYW Summit 2025 Dates** : 3 – 6 November 2025
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 3rd March 2025**

**Also Check:[One Young World Summit Leading Scholarship 2025 in Germany (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/one-young-world-summit-leading-scholarship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Roche UK Scholarship covers all the expenses to attend a One Young World Summit 2025 in Munich, Germany. The Scholarship covers:
  * Access to the One Young World Summit in Munich.
  * Hotel Accommodation (3-6 Nov 2025)
  * Meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
  * Round Airfare Ticket to/from Munich
  * Airport Transfer
  * Public transport in Munich
  * Watch Short Video **[Highlight of the 2023 OYW Summit](https://youtu.be/vcWW1TLtz7U?si=2UIwUBy5uFa01y8H)**.

**Also Check:[Korean Youth Summit 2025 (KYS) Cultural Exchange Program](https://opportunitiescorners.com/korean-youth-summit/)**
### **Objectives of the Roche Program**
The Roche Scholarship is specifically for NHS young leaders who have shown a strong commitment to developing and implementing sustainable healthcare innovations. Examples may include:
  * Tackling the causes and effects of health inequalities.
  * Creating or implementing innovative methods of treatment or diagnosis to enhance the patient care pathway.
  * Reducing emissions or burdens on natural resources, such as through decarbonizing healthcare supply chains.

**Also Check:[Global Peace Summit in Sydney, Australia 2025 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/global-peace-summit-in-sydney/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Nationals of all countries are eligible to apply for this scholarship, but must:
    * Be based in the United Kingdom.
    * Hold a position in the NHS or an organization working in partnership with the NHS.
  * Age: 18-35 at the time of One Young World Summit 2025.
  * Track record of generating impactful and innovative solutions.
  * Demonstrated commitment to sustainable innovation in healthcare.

**Also Check:[Universal Youth Leadership Summit 2025 in MALDIVES (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/universal-youth-leadership-summit-in-maldives/)**
### **How to Apply for the Roche Scholarship 2025 to Attend a One Young World Summit 2025?**
Applicants have to apply online from the One Young World Website. The Link to the official website is below.
[APPLY FOR THE ROCHE OYW SCHOLARSHIP 2025](https://www.oneyoungworld.com/scholarships/roche/2025)
