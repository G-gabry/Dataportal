The applications are now open for the **Global Leadership and Innovation Summit Turkey** **2026**. This international summit is organized by the **Voice for Rights International Association** , where young leaders unite to shape the future. The GLIS 2026 Summit is open to candidates from **around the world**. The program will run from **17th April to 20th April 2026**. No formal english language test is required. The summit brings together exceptional young leaders for three days of leadership, innovation, and cultural exchange. The program focuses on Youth leadership, innovation, and Technology. Below are more details about the requirements, financial benefits, and the application process.
## **Details About Global Leadership and Innovation Summit Turkey 2026 (GLIS)**
  * **Host Country** : Turkey
  * **Location** : TBD
  * **Duration** : 3 Days
  * **Dates** : 17th April to 20th April 2026
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 16 Feb 2026**

**Also Check:[Stowers Summer Program 2026 Kansas, USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/stowers-summer-program/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Round-trip airfare (will be provided to the Top Delegate)
  * 100% waiver of the Conference fee to 15 Delegates.
  * Awards recognizing their efforts
  * Accommodation for the whole period of the Program for 15 Delegates.
  * Certificate
  * Meals
  * Conference Kit
  * Site Seeing
  * Cultural Exchange Program
  * Celebration Party

**Also Check:[CRG Research Training Program 2026 in Spain (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/crg-research-training-program/)**
### **Conference Topics**
  * **Youth Leadership**
    * Exploring how young leaders can create social impact, foster positive change, and contribute to a more peaceful and innovative world.
  * **Innovation & Technology for Sustainable Development**
    * Understanding how technology, creativity, and youth-led initiatives drive progress toward global sustainability.

### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Must be between **16 and 35 years old**.
  * Must be passionate about driving positive **social change**.
  * Should be currently engaged in **social or educational activities** at any level (local, national, or international).
  * Highly motivated students with **no prior social experience** are still encouraged and eligible to apply.

### **Application Fee**
  * $19.99+tax (Charged by the VFRI)

**Also Check:[Summer Programs in Europe in 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/summer-programs-in-europe-in-2025/)**
### **How to Apply for the Global Leadership and Innovation Summit Turkey 2026?**
Complete the online application form and pay the application fee. The link to the official website is below.
