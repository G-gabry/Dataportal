The applications are now open for the Rotary Peace Fellowship Program 2027/28. It is a **Fully Funded** **Master’s** scholarship and a **Certification** Program. The Fellowship is open to **all the Nationals** from all around the world. For 2027/28 rotary will award **170[fully funded fellowships](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/fellowships/)**. The **Objective** of this Fellowship is to teach participants about the Peace and Development. Applicants can pursue **Master’s** Programs from Universities in Australia, England, Japan, Sweden, and the USA. The Certificate Programs are available in Turkey, Uganda, and India. The **Duration** of the Master’s Program is from 15 to 24 Months and the Professional Development Certificate Course is one year. More details are below.
## **Details About Rotary Peace Fellowship 2027/28**
  * **Host Countries** : Australia, England, Japan, Sweden, USA, Turkiye, Uganda, India
  * **Fellowship Level** : Master’s and Certificate Program
  * **Financial Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Duration** : 12 Months, 15 Months, 24 Months
  * **Deadline: 15th May 2026**

**Also Check:[NTU Global Connect Fellowship 2026 in Singapore 🇸🇬 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/ntu-global-connect-fellowship-in-singapore/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Tuition Covered
  * Room and Board included
  * Round Airfare Tickets
  * All Internship Expenses
  * Field-study expenses covered
  * Health insurance included

**Also Check:[Bloomberg Fellowship 2026 New York, USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/bloomberg-fellowship/)**
### **List of Universities For Master’s Degree Programs for Rotary Peace Fellowship 2027/28**
Candidates will complete their Master’s Degree program at one of these universities.
  * Duke University and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, USA
  * International Christian University, Tokyo, Japan
  * University of Bradford, Bradford, England
  * University of Queensland, Brisbane, Australia
  * Uppsala University, Uppsala, Sweden
  * Learn more about **[Rotary Master Programs](https://www.rotary.org/en/our-programs/peace-fellowships-masters-degree-programs)**

### **List of Universities For Professional Development Certificate Programs for Rotary Peace Fellowship 2027/28**
  * Makerere University, Kampala, Uganda
  * Bahçeşehir University (BAU), Istanbul, Türkiye
  * Symbiosis International University, Pune, India
  * Learn more about **[Rotary Certificate Programs](https://www.rotary.org/en/our-programs/peace-fellowships-certificate-program)**

### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * **Nationality** : The Fellowship is open to all nationals.
  * **Academic Excellence:** Candidates should demonstrate strong academic performance.
  * **Educational Background:** A bachelor’s degree in a related field is required.
  * **Professional Experience:** 3 years of relevant work experience in the master’s program and 5 years of experience in Certificate programs.
  * **Leadership Potential:** Candidates must demonstrate the potential for leadership in their field.
  * **Time Gap:** A minimum **three (3) year** gap is required between the completion of the candidate’s most recent academic degree program (undergraduate or graduate) and their intended fellowship start date.

### **How to Apply for the Rotary Peace Fellowship 2027/28?**
The Rotary Peace Fellowship application is now open! The 2027/28 Rotary Peace Fellowship application is available **[online](https://my.rotary.org/en/peace-fellowship-application)**. The official link to the website is below.
