The Applications are open to apply for the **Fiction Writers Program 2025**. Winners will get a **Fully Funded Trip to Greece**. The Fiction Writers Program invites writers, professionals, and students from **all around the world** to Participate in a **week-long** program at the Costa Navarino, Messinia, Greece. Participants will spend **8 days** in Greece. The Fiction Writers Program is open to applicants from all around the world. During the Program, fellows will engage in workshops, and craft sessions and will meet with other leading writers of the industry. There is **no application fee** and the Oxbelly covers all the costs of the Participants including **Travel** and **Accommodation** in Greece. The **objective** of this writing program is to support exceptional narratives in all genres. Writers can select any subject or topic, but it should be original. Below are more details about the type of stories, the benefits, and the application process.
## **Details About Fiction Writers Program 2025**
  * **Host Country** : Greece
  * **Location** : Costa Navarino, Messinia, Greece
  * **Duration** : 8 Days
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 22nd January 2025**

**Also Check:[Merck Innovation Summer Camp 2025 in Germany (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/merck-innovation-cup-summer-camp/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
This fully-funded writing program offers:
  * Travel Expenses to/from Greece
  * Accommodation in Greece
  * No cost to Apply
  * Invitation Letter

**Also Check:[Aalto University Summer Program 2025 in Finland (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/aalto-university-international-summer-program/)**
### **Number of Fellowships**
The Oxbelly Fiction Writers Program will select **10 fellows** for its 2025 edition.
### **Program Dates in Greece**
It is 8 Day Program in Greece. The 2025 Oxbelly Retreat will take place **June 28 – July 6, 2025.**
**Also Check:[AYFN JAPAN Summer Culture Camp 2025 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/ayfn-japan-summer-culture-camp/)**
### **What Type of Work You can Submit?**
You may submit any type of fictional prose, such as short stories or novel manuscripts. Poetry, lyrics, screenplays, or theatrical plays are not accepted.
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Open to Greek and international applicants.
  * **Age Limit:** Applicants must be 18 years of age or older.
  * **Originality:** Submitted work must be the applicant’s original, unpublished creation.
  * **Prior Publication Restriction:** Submitted work should not have been previously published online, in print, or in any other format.
  * Emerging writers of novels or short stories, including unpublished writers and students, can apply.
  * Applicants can submit any kind of story in any subject.

**Also Check:[University of Hong Kong Summer Program 2025 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/university-of-hong-kong-summer-program/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Valid Passport.
  * Writing Sample.
  * National Identity Card.

### **How to Apply for the Fiction Writers Program 2025?**
Apply online using the link provided on the official website. Enter your email address to begin the application process. The Link to the official website is below.
[APPLY FOR THE FICTION WRITERS PROGRAM](https://www.oxbelly.com/fiction-writers-program)
