The applications are now open for the **Sumitomo Electric Global Internship 2026** in **Japan**. It is a **Fully Funded Summer Internship** for International students from **around the world** and is open to **students holding a BS, BA, or Master’s degree**. There is **no application Fee,** and the Sumitomo Electric Global Internship will take place in **Osaka** and **Yokohama** , Japan. The Duration of the Internship is **10** to **12** **Weeks**. Moreover, IELTS is **not** required. The Program **aims** to provide Practical work experience at the R&D labs or corporate **headquarters** of Sumitomo Electric Industries. Dreaming of a career in Japan? Consider the 10 or 12-week Sumitomo Electric Global Internship Program.
## **About Sumitomo Electric Global Internship 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Japan
  * **Location** : Osaka and Yokohama
  * **Duration** : 10 Weeks and 12 Weeks
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 6th February 2026**

**Also Check:[List of Summer Programs in Japan 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/list-of-summer-programs-in-japan/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Round-trip Airfare from your university’s country to Japan
  * Accommodation
  * Meals
  * Rail pass (if applicable)
  * Travel Insurance
  * Weekly stipend to assist with living expenses in Japan.
  * Full-time roles may be offered after the internship.

**Also Check:[NIMS Internship 2026 in Japan | Fully Funded | 100 Interns](https://opportunitiescorners.com/nims-internship/)**
### **Academic Background of Applicant**
  * Industrial Engineering
  * Electrical
  * Electronic Engineering
  * Material Science
  * Human Resources Development
  * Physics or Engineering
  * Optics and Optical Fibers

**Also Check:[OIST Research Internship in Japan (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/oist-research-internship-in-japan/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Candidates from all around the world are eligible for the Sumitomo Electric Global Internship.
  * Students currently enrolled in an accredited university seeking a BS or MS degree.
  * Basic Knowledge of Microsoft Excel, MS Word, and PowerPoint.
  * Excellent oral and written communication skills in English.

**Also Check:[OIST Japan Internship Program 2025 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/oist-japan-internship/)**
### **How to Apply for the Japan Sumitomo Electric Global Internship 2026?**
Applicants can apply online from the official website. There is no application fee. The Link to the official website is below. For more Fully Funded International Internships, Kindly check our **[Internship Category](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)**.
