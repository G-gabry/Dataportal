2026 is dedicated to the year of opportunities. Paris is calling the world’s young leaders. The Applications are now open for the **Youth World Summit 2026, Paris, France**. It is a **fully funded** international youth summit taking place in Paris from **11–14 June 2026**. The program aims to bring together students, volunteers, mid-career professionals, young leaders, and change-makers for a dialogue, learning, and collaboration on youth policy, leadership, peace, and sustainable development. It will empower young people to engage with global policy frameworks, strengthen leadership capacities, and build cross-border networks that extend well beyond the summit experience. Candidates from around the world are eligible to apply. IELTS is also **not** required. There are **100 scholarships** available. **[Apply now for the YWS Paris 2026](https://thecips.org/yws2026/ref/10/)**.
## **About Youth World Summit 2026 Paris, France**
  * **Host Country** : France
  * **Location** : Paris
  * **Duration** : 4 Days
  * **Dates** : 11–14 June 2026
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 10 April 2026**

**Also Check:[One Future Conference 2026 in Canada (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/one-future-conference-in-canada/)**
### **Number of Seats**
  * **Total** : 250
    * **Fully Funded** : 40
    * **Partially Funded** : 60
    * **Self-Funded** : 100
    * **Forum Access Only** : 50

### **Financial Benefits**
**The Fully Funded Scholarship Covers:**
  * Round Trip Flight
  * Shared Accommodation
  * Meals
  * Airport Transfer
  * Local Transport
  * Full Conference Access
  * Invitation Letter
  * Exclusive visits to UN Headquarters, CERN, and policy institutions
  * Global Leadership Certificate
  * Premium Training Kit and Event Merchandise.

**The Partially Funded Scholarship Covers:**
  * Everything mentioned except the Airfare.

**Note** : The application fee is **Refundable** upon selection (For fully and Partially Funded).
### **Guaranteed Participation Categories (Direct Selection)**
  * **[Forum Access](https://thecips.org/yws2026/ref/10/)** : This Category covers everything except Airfare and Accommodation. Direct selection, without going through the selection and interview process.
  * **[Self Funded](https://thecips.org/yws2026/ref/10/)** : This Category covers everything except Airfare. Direct selection, without going through the selection and interview process.
  * **[Invitation Letter](https://thecips.org/yws2026/ref/10/)** : For participants who only need official documentation, an invitation letter to support their visa application.

### **Themes of the YWS Paris 2026**
  * Youth Policy & Global Governance
  * Peace, Security & Conflict Transformation
  * Climate Action & Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  * Leadership, Diplomacy & Global Citizenship
  * Youth Participation in Decision-Making

### **Eligibility Criteria**
The **Youth World Summit 2026** is inclusive of diverse backgrounds and experiences. Here is the breakdown of who is eligible to join:
  * **Age Requirement:** Open to young people aged **17 to 46**.
  * **Global Reach:** Applicants from **all countries** are welcome.
  * **Student Status:** Includes undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students.
  * **Career Level:** Open to young professionals and early-career leaders.
  * **Diverse Roles:** We welcome entrepreneurs, researchers, activists, and volunteers.
  * **Academic/Professional Background:** Individuals from **all fields** are encouraged to apply.

**_Nationality, academic discipline, or professional sector is not a barrier—diversity is a core value of the summit._**
### **How to Apply for the Youth World Summit 2026 Paris?**
Candidates can apply online through the official website by selecting their preferred category and paying the application fee. Please note that the application fee is **fully refundable** for candidates selected for fully or Partially Funded seats. The link is provided below.
