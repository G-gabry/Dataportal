It’s a Polish Time! The time is here to apply for the Scholarships to Study in Poland Universities. Applications are open for the **University of Silesia Poland Scholarship 2024/25** for the **winter academic** intake to pursue First-cycle, Second-cycle, and long-cycle studies. In recent years, Poland has become an attractive choice for international students because of High-Quality education at a low price. The University of Silesia is a Public university located in Katowice, Silesia Province. It has more than 80 degree programs and 200+ majors across various fields.
Poland is located in the heart of Europe right in the Central European Location making it easy for international students to travel to other European countries as well. The Polish Government has also announced the **[Stefan Banach Scholarship](https://opportunitiescorners.com/poland-government-stefan-banach-scholarship/)** Program Funded by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the NAWA. This scholarship covers tuition, travel, and a monthly stipend. The University of Silesia has several scholarships for international students. More details about the scholarship, admission requirements, and the application process are given below.
## **Details About University of Silesia Poland Scholarship 2024/25**
  * **Host Country** : Poland
  * **University Name** : University of Silesia
  * **Degree Level** : First-cycle, Second-cycle, and long-cycle studies
  * **Deadline: 30th September 2024**

**Also Check:[BS MS PhD Scholarships in Poland for 2024-25 (Apply Now)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/bs-ms-phd-scholarships-in-poland/)**
### **What is First Cycle, Second Cycle, and Long Cycle Studies?**
  * **First-cycle studies:** This is typically equivalent to a Bachelor’s degree program.
  * **Second-cycle studies:** This refers to Master’s degree programs.
  * **Long-cycle studies:** This is a special category that combines first and second-cycle programs into a single, extended program.

### **Study in English at the University of Silesia**
In 2024-25, the University of Silesia offers a wide range of academic programs in English. All the classes including lectures, seminars will be taught in English Language. MA/MSc theses are written in English. All degree programs completely taught in English.
