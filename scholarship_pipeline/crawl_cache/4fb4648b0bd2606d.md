The Applications are now open for the **GIST Global Internship 2026 in South Korea**. It is a **[Fully Funded International Summer Internship](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)** at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology, South Korea. The GIST Global Intern Program is open to the **Undergraduates** and **master’s** students. The **Duration** of the Internship is **8 weeks** and is open to all international students from **all around the world**. Moreover, there is **no application fee,** and IELTS is also **not** required. The **Objective** of the GIST Global Intern Program is to work on research projects in the laboratory at GIST University during the Summer. The Internship will start in **July 2026**. Below you will find more details about the application process, financial benefits, Internship areas, and the requirements.
## **Details About GIST Global Internship 2026**
  * **Host Country** : South Korea
  * **University** : GIST
  * **Duration:** 8 Weeks
  * **Internship Month** : July to August 2026
  * **Financial Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: February 27, 2025**

**Also Check:[KAIST-X Summer Internship 2026 in South Korea (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/kaist-x-summer-internship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Airfare
  * Accommodation
  * Stipend
  * Korean Language Class
  * Cultural Class
  * Special Lectures
  * Culture Exploration
  * Make new friends and meet fellow GIP Interns from all over the world.

**Also Check:[ITRI Taiwan Summer Internship 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/itri-taiwan-summer-internship/)**
### **Internship Areas**
The GIST Global Intern Program aims to develop participants’ knowledge and skills in the following areas:
  * School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
  * School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
  * Department of Semiconductor Engineering
  * School of Materials Science and Engineering
  * School of Mechanical and Robotics Engineering
  * School of Environment and Energy Engineering
  * School of Life Sciences
  * Institute of Integrated Technology
  * Department of Physics and Photon Science
  * Department of Chemistry
  * Department of Biomedical Science & Engineering
  * Artificial Intelligence Graduate School

**Also Check:[Sumitomo Electric Global Internship 2026 in Japan (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/sumitomo-electric-global-internship/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The Internship is open to all Nationals.
  * Citizens from all around the world are eligible for the GIST South Korea Internship.
  * Open to international undergraduate seniors or Master’s degree students.
  * A minimum GPA of 3.0 (on a 4.5 scale) or equivalent.
  * Students who are citizens of an English-speaking country or whose primary language of instruction at their university is English may submit a copy of their passport or a certificate stating “Medium of Instruction is English”.

**Also Check:[GRIPS Summer Research Internship 2026 in China (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/grips-summer-internship-in-china/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Official Degree Certificates (e.g., diploma, enrollment/graduation certificate)
  * Official Transcripts (in English)
  * Letter of Recommendation
  * English Proficiency Proof

### **How to Apply for the GIST Global Internship 2026?**
Applicants have to apply online from the official website. Create an account with your email address. The Link to the official website is below. For more fully funded Good International Internships, kindly check out the **[Internship Category](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)**.
