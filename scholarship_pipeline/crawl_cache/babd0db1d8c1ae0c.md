Summer in Finland. The applications are now open for the **AScI International Summer Program 2026**. It is a **[Fully Funded Summer Internship](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)** for International students from all around the world. This Summer Program is open to **Bachelor’s** and **Master’s** students to join a **three-month** research Program to work on the research Projects. Around **50 applicants** from all over the world will join the Aalto Science Institute (AScI) International Summer Research Program. Make the most of your summer in Finland. The Program will run for **12 weeks** , from **June to August 2026**. There is no application fee. Below you will find more information on the Financial Benefits, Internship Areas, and the application process.
## **Details About Finland AScI International Summer Program 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Finland
  * **Internship Location** : Aalto University Otaniemi campus
  * **Internship Dates** : June-August 2026
  * **No of Positions** : 50
  * **Financial Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 31st January 2026**

**Also Check:[John Innes Centre Summer School 2026, UK (Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/john-innes-centre-summer-school/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Airfare Travel Tickets
  * Paid Vacation
  * 2000+ EUR/Month to Cover
    * Housing
    * Lunch
  * Social events

**Also Check:[GRIPS Summer Research Internship 2026 in China (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/grips-summer-internship-in-china/)**
### **Research Areas**
Approximately 50 summer research positions are available across 7 departments for both bachelor’s and master’s students. **[Explore the Project List here](https://www.aalto.fi/en/aalto-science-institute-asci/asci-international-summer-research-programme-project-list)**.
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The Program is open to all the Nationals. Both Europeans and non-Europeans can apply.
  * Must be an Undergraduate or graduate student.
  * Internships are available in various fields, including Science, Engineering (Chemical, Electrical), Business, Arts, Design, and Architecture.
  * IELTS is not required.

**Also Check:[International Summer School 2026 | University of Oslo, Norway (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/university-of-oslo-international-summer-school/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Motivation letter
  * CV
  * Official Transcript
  * A letter of recommendation from a professor or supervisor.
  * Any other document that can support your application.

**Also Check:[Stowers Summer Program 2026, Kansas, USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/stowers-summer-program/)**
### **How to Apply for the AScI International Summer Program 2026?**
The application link is below. Please ly check out our **[Internships Category](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)**.
[APPLY FOR AALTO UNIVERSITY AScI SUMMER INTERNSHIP](https://www.aalto.fi/en/aalto-science-institute-asci/how-to-apply-for-the-asci-international-summer-research-programme)
