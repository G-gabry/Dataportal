The Applications are open to apply for the **University of Eastern Finland (UEF) Scholarship** for the academic Year **2025**. International students from **all around the world** can apply for the **Bachelor** , and **Master** Programs at the University of Eastern Finland. Nearly **500 student** Places are available in **25 programs**. Non-European citizens can apply for the Scholarships offered by the University of Eastern Finland. The Studies in these programs will begin in the **autumn of 2025**. The University of Eastern Finland boasts a diverse student body, welcoming approximately 2,000 international students from over 100 countries. It is a public university. Scholarship applications should be submitted simultaneously with applications for admission to Bachelor’s and Master’s programs. More details are given below.
## **Details About University of Eastern Finland (UEF) Scholarship 2025**
  * **Host Country** : Finland
  * **University Name** : University of Eastern Finland (UEF)
  * **Degree Level** : Bachelors, Masters
  * **Financial Benefits** : Tuition Waiver
  * **Deadline: 22nd January 2025**

**Also Check:[Erasmus Mundus GLOBAL STUDIES Scholarship 2025 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/erasmus-mundus-global-studies-scholarship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
There are three types of scholarships available for Non-Europeans:
  * **UEF Scholarship:** A 100% Tuition Waiver
  * **Tuition Waiver:** Up to 50% of the tuition fee
  * **Finnish Proficiency tuition waivers:** Applied for during studies at UEF.
  * **Note** : Studies are free of charge for Finnish citizens and all EU/EEA and Swiss citizens.

**Also Check:[Joint Japan World Bank Scholarship 2025 (Fully Funded) JJWBGSP](https://opportunitiescorners.com/world-bank-scholarship/)**
### **Available Bachelor’s Degree Programs**
  * Social Sciences
  * Lifelong Learning and Sustainable Development
  * Information Technology
  * Sustainable Forest Bioeconomy

### **Available Master Degree Programs**
  * Accounting and Finance
  * Biology of Environmental Change
  * Biomedicine
  * Digital Marketing and Analytics
  * Early Language Education for Intercultural Communication
  * English Language and Culture
  * Environmental and Ecosystem Ecology
  * Environmental Health and Technology
  * Environmental Policy and Law
  * Forestry
  * International Business and Sales Management
  * Information Technology (IMPIT)
  * Linguistic Data Sciences
  * Medical Physics
  * Pedagogy and Teaching for Sustainability
  * Photonics
  * Public Health
  * Research Chemists
  * Sustainability Leadership
  * Tourism Marketing and Management
  * Toxicology

**Also Check:[University of Paris Saclay Scholarship 2025 in France (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/university-of-paris-saclay-scholarship/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Both International and Finnish Citizens can apply.
  * Applicants must have completed a secondary education.
  * Both Finnish and international applicants holding a relevant Bachelor’s degree are eligible to apply for admission to Master’s degree programs.
  * Meet the Specific Program requirements.

### **English Language Requirements**
An English Language Test is not required if the applicant has completed a Bachelors Degree from a University located in:
Australia, Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Botswana, Cameroon, Canada, Dominica, Eritrea, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Gambia, Ghana, Grenada, Guyana, Hong Kong, India, Jamaica, Kenya, Lesotho, Liberia, Malawi, Namibia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Philippines, Rwanda, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and Grenadines, Sierra Leone, Singapore, South-Africa, Switzerland, Tanzania, Trinidad and Tobago, Uganda, the United Kingdom, the United States, Zambia, Zimbabwe.

### **How to Apply for the University of Eastern Finland (UEF) Scholarship 2025?**
Applicants have to apply online via Joint application. Applicants can submit their applications online through the national **Studyinfo.fi** portal. The Link to the official website is below.
  * **For Bachelor Application:[Visit Here](https://www.uef.fi/en/international-bachelors-degree-programmes)**
  * **For Master Applications:[Visit Here](https://www.uef.fi/en/international-masters-degree-programmes)**
