The applications are now open for the **Thailand International Postgraduate Program (TIPP)** 2026. It is a **[fully funded scholarship](https://opportunitiescorners.com/)** for international students to pursue a **Master’s degree** program from the Thai universities. The TIPP scholarship is established by the Royal Thai Government. It is managed by the **Thailand International Cooperation Agency (TICA)** under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The scholarship covers all the expenses, and there is **no application fee** to apply. The **duration** of the program is from **one** to **two** years. It is a great opportunity to gain experience from Thailand’s educational institutes and deepen their understanding of Thailand, its rich culture, and the hospitality of the Thai people. More details are below.
## **Details About Thailand International Postgraduate Program (TIPP) 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Thailand
  * **Location** : Thai Universities
  * **Program** : Masters
  * **Duration** : One or Two Years
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 23rd February 2026**

**Also Check:[Erasmus Mundus META4.0 Scholarship 2026/28 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/erasmus-mundus-meta4-0-scholarship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Scholarship covers:
  * Return Airfare
  * Monthly Stipend
  * Visa Fees
  * Tuition Fee
  * Insurance
  * Airport Pickup
  * Settlement, books, and thesis allowance.

**Also Check:[Islamic Development Bank IsDB Scholarship 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/islamic-development-bank-isdb-scholarship-2025/)**
### **List of courses for the 2026 Thailand International Postgraduate Program (TIPP)**
Check the Eligible Courses, program name, and eligible universities for the **[TIPP here](https://tica-thaigov.mfa.go.th/en/content/tipp-2027)**.
### **TIPP 2026 Themes**
  * Sufficiency Economy Philosophy: SEP
  * Public Health
  * Agriculture and Food Security
  * Climate Change and Environmental Issues
  * Bio-Circular-Green Economy: BCG Model
  * Other topics related to the United Nations Sustainable Goals.

**Also Check:[CRG Research Training Program 2026 in Spain (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/crg-research-training-program/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
To qualify for TIPP 2026, candidates generally must meet these criteria:
  * You **must** be nominated by your home country’s National Focal Point (e.g., Ministry of Foreign Affairs or Ministry of Education)
  * **Employment:** Preference is given to those working in the government sector or development-related fields
  * **Education:** Must hold a Bachelor’s degree in a related field
  * **Age:** Usually must be **under 45 or 50 years old** (depending on the specific course)
  * **English Proficiency:** Good command of English.

### **Application Process**
Applicants can apply online from the TICA official website. Please read the **[complete application Guidelines](https://image.mfa.go.th/mfa/0/GH2PYnujXi/%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A3/Guidelines_for_TIPP_2026_2.pdf)**. The link to the official website is below.
