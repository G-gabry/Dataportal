Study in Finland Universities and apply for the **Finland Government Scholarship 2024**. The scholarships are funded by the **Ministry of Education and Culture**. Anyone from any country can apply that exists outside the EU/EEA. Finland Scholarships are for all international students. It is one of the fastest-growing international hubs in Europe. The **Joint Applications are open** for international students. All degree programs are available via the joint application.
The Joint application system means you can apply for **up to 6** degree programs in a single application. Finnish Higher Education institutions offer over **500** English-taught degree programs. It is home to some of the best universities in the world and offers a great variety of new and exciting careers for graduates. The Application process and requirements are given below.

## **About Finland Government Scholarship**
  * **Host Country:** Finland
  * **Degree Level:** Bachelor, Master, PhD
  * **Financial Coverage:** Funded
  * **Deadline: January 17, 2024, for the September 2024 intake**

This call is open for the **September 2024 intake.** All degree programs available via the joint application share the same application period.
**Also read:[Top European Government Scholarships in 2024 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/european-government-scholarships/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
  * The Finland Scholarships Covers: First-year tuition fee and a relocation grant of 5000€ as a minimum
  * All the universities in Finland offer various percentages of the **Tuition Fee Waiver Scholarship** OR the **Tuition Fee + Monthly Stipend** Scholarship.
  * Read about the Funding details **[Here](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/scholarships/finland-scholarships)**
  * Also, Check this section for Bachelor, and Masters Scholarships (**[here](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/scholarships/bachelors-and-masters-scholarships)**)
  * **Note** : There is **No Separate Application** Required for the Finland Government Scholarship. By default, your application will be considered for a scholarship.
  * There is no application fee, applying is always Free.

### **List of Participating Universities**
Finland has **13** universities and **22** universities of applied sciences.
  * Aalto University
  * University of Helsinki
  * University of Eastern Finland
  * University of Jyväskylä
  * University of Lapland
  * LUT University
  * University of Oulu
  * Hanken School of Economics
  * University of the Arts Helsinki
  * Tampere University
  * University of Turku
  * University of Vaasa
  * Åbo Akademi University

Find more about Universities **[here](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/universities-list-view)**.

  * Arts

### **Application Process for the Finland Joint Application System 2024**
Open to international students from non-EU/EEA countries. The application process is done online at **[Studyinfo.fi joint application](https://studyinfo.fi/konfo/en/sivu/joint-application-to-higher-education)**
At the Studyinfo.fi application portal you can find for example:
  * **[Database of English-taught Degree Programs](https://studyinfo.fi/konfo/en/haku/?opetuskieli=oppilaitoksenopetuskieli_4&order=desc&size=20&sort=score)**
  * **[Application process Explained](https://studyinfo.fi/konfo/en/sivu/finnish-application-system)**
  * **[Answers to many commonly asked questions](https://studyinfo.fi/konfo/en/sivu/faq-about-applying-to-higher-education)**

If you need advice on your application, get in touch with the Admissions Services of the university you are applying to. You can find their **[contact details at Studyinfo.fi](https://studyinfo.fi/konfo/en/sivu/admissions-services-of-finnish-higher-education-institutions)** , or on the **[universities’ admissions info pages](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/universities-list-view)**.
[VISIT HERE FOR THE APPLICATION PROCESS](https://www.studyinfinland.fi/admissions/bachelors-and-masters-admissions)
You will get your study residence permit in 10 days as Finland has launched a fast-track service called D Visa. Read about the **[Finland Student Visa Process.](https://opportunitiescorners.com/finland-student-visa-process-2023/)**
