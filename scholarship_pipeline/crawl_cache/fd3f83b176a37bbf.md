Applications are open for the **World Bank LCR Inclusive Internship Program 2024**. This is a **Paid** internship by the World Bank Group for the students to solve their biggest development challenges. The duration of the internship is **2 months**. Interns will work either in the World Bank HQ in Washington D.C. or in their own countries’ World Bank offices. Interns will be paid on an hourly basis as set by the country. The World Bank receives about **1500 applications** each year for the Inclusive Internship Program.
Through this internship, interns will get the benefits of onboarding and training, networking, mentoring, and coaching from World Bank staff and being a part of one of the most reputable development banks in the world. You can also apply for the **[World Bank Junior Professional Associates](https://opportunitiescorners.com/world-bank-junior-professional-associates/)**. It is a three-year program for the freshers. More details about the World Bank LCR Inclusive Internship are given below.
## **Details About World Bank LCR Inclusive Internship Program**
  * **Sponsored by** : World Bank
  * **Duration** : 2 Months
  * **Location** : Washington D.C. or a World Bank office in an **[LAC country](http://lanic.utexas.edu/subject/countries/)**
  * **Deadline: 22nd July 2024**

After the completion of the internship, interns are encouraged to apply for the **[World Bank Junior Professional Associates (JPA) Program](https://opportunitiescorners.com/world-bank-junior-professional-associates/)** and the [**World Bank Group Young Professionals Program**](https://opportunitiescorners.com/world-bank-group-young-professionals-program/).
### **Is this a Paid Internship?**
Yes. It is a Paid internship program. Interns will receive a Stipend on an Hourly basis.
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * A graduate-level student currently enrolled, or a recent graduate (Master’s or PhD).
  * The internship is open to people from all backgrounds and ethnicities.
  * The age should not be more than 30 years.
  * At least 1-2 years of relevant work experience (It may include previous internships, paid, unpaid, volunteer work).
  * Excellent English and Portuguese communication skills.
  * Strong interest in international development.

**Also Check:[UNHCR Internship (Travel, Stipend, Living Support Covered)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/unhcr-internship/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Curriculum Vitae (CV)-maximum 4 pages
  * Statement of Interest- maximum 1 page

**Also Check:[WIPO Internship Roster 2025 in Switzerland (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/wipo-internship-roster/)**
### **How to Apply for the Latin America and Caribbean Region (LCR) Inclusive Internship Program (IIP) 2024?**
Please apply online from the World Bank website. The link to the official website is given below.
[VISIT HERE TO APPLY FOR THE WORLD BANK LCR INTERNSHIP](https://worldbankgroup.csod.com/ats/careersite/JobDetails.aspx?id=27963&site=1)
