WFP Stands for the World Food Programme. It is the leading organization within the United Nations that provides food assistance worldwide. It is working to fight and remove hunger worldwide. They have One Goal- achieve Zero Hunger. The applications are open to apply for the **Fully Funded** **WFP Internship**. An internship with WFP is a great opportunity to gain experience in how to achieve Zero Hunger. Applicants from all around the world can apply. Students, recent graduates, and enrolled can apply for the WFP Internship Programme.
To support interns, WFP will also cover your **Travel Air Tickets** along with this they will also provide you with a **$1000/Month Stipend** to cover the cost of living and other expenses. The **Duration** of the WFP Internship is two and eight months. WFP has been awarded a **Gold Standard Internship** by the University of Oxford for the support and the quality of the internship. More details about the internship, requirements, and eligibility criteria are given below.
## **Details About WFP Internship Program**
  * **Organization Name** : WFP (World Food Programme)
  * **Internship Location** : Multiple Countries
  * **Who can Apply** : Bachelors, Masters, Graduated students
  * **Benefits** : Travel Allowance, Monthly Stipend
  * **Deadline: Open all Year**

**Also Check:[IATA Internship Program 2024 (Allowance and Travel Expenses)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/iata-internship-program/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Currently enrolled in a recognized university or graduated in the last six months.
  * Open to all students from around the world.
  * Completed at least 2 years of undergraduate studies.
  * Bachelors, Masters, or Graduated students can apply.
  * Interested in learning and taking a first step in the humanitarian sector.

**Also Check:[UNOPS Internship 2024 (Monthly Stipend and Travel Fund)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/unops-internship/)**
### **Support for Interns**
All the Interns will receive the following benefits:
  * Round Trip Airfare Travel Tickets from your home to the Internship location for nationals of developing countries.
  * A $1000 Monthly stipend to cover your expenses.
  * Invitation letter for Visa support.
  * You **do not need to provide any IELTS or TOEFL Test results.**
  * Applying for a WFP Internship is Free, and no application fee is required.

**Also Check:[WMO Internship 2024 in Geneva, Switzerland (1538 Euros/Month)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/wmo-internship/)**
### **Things to Keep in Mind**
Once you have been chosen for the internship, you will be asked to provide your recent university records such as proof of enrollment and transcripts.
Before you start your internship, you will be asked for proof of medical insurance. WFP will provide **Visa support** and can support you with the required documents.
### **Area of Expertise**
  * Programme Management
  * Logistics
  * Finance
  * Human Resources
  * Information Technology
  * Communication
  * Research
  * Nutrition
  * Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene
  * Education
  * Gender
  * Youth
  * Coordination
  * Policy
  * Advocacy
  * Emergency Operation
  * Supply Chain
  * Security
  * Resource Management
  * Business Support
  * Communication
  * and much more.

**Also Check:[WIPO Internship Roster 2024 in Switzerland (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/wipo-internship-roster/)**
### **How to Apply for the WFP Internship Programme?**
Applying for the Internship is an easy process. If you already have a CV it’s good. If not then make a Good CV. The official Link of the WFP internship is given below.
[VISIT HERE TO APPLY FOR THE WFP INTERNSHIP](https://www.wfp.org/careers/internships)
