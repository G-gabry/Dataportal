The applications are now open for the **STScI SASP Summer Program 2026** in the **USA**. Each Year, STScI invites students to its Space Astronomy Summer Program (SASP). It is a **[Fully Funded Summer Internship](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)** for International students **from all around the world**. Participants will arrive at the STScI campus in **Baltimore, Maryland** , USA. The Duration of the STScI Summer Internship is **9 weeks**. The Summer Internship will run from June 1 to July 31, 2026. There is **no application fee,** and applicants from all nationalities can apply. The STScI Summer Internship is open to **Undergraduate** and **Graduate** students. The STScI will assist International students in obtaining a work visa if necessary. More details are below.
## **Details About STScI SASP Summer Program 2026**
  * **Host Country** : USA
  * **Internship Location** : STScI campus in Baltimore, Maryland
  * **Duration** : 9 Weeks
  * **Program Dates** : 2nd June to 1st August 2025
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 23rd January 2026**

**Also Check:[List of Summer Programs in Japan 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/list-of-summer-programs-in-japan/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Weekly Stipend
  * Work Visa
  * Round Airfare Tickets
  * Accommodation
  * Access to a Laptop and a Computer during the Internship
  * You can use the Metro if you would like to travel to nearby cities such as Washington, D.C., Philadelphia, or New York City.

**Also Check:[BDSY Summer Program 2026 at Yale, USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/bdsy-summer-program/)**
### **Internship Areas**
  * Data Reduction and Interpretation
  * Software Development
  * Software Engineering
  * Instrument Calibration and Support
  * Scientific Writing
  * Public Outreach
  * Strong Interest in:
    * Space-based Astronomy
    * Engineering
    * Public Outreach (Other candidates may be considered on an individual basis)

**Also Check:[Purdue University Summer Fellowship 2026 in USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/purdue-university-summer-fellowship/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The Program is open to all the Nationals.
  * Both International and U.S. Citizens are eligible to apply.
  * Open to International Undergraduate students and beginning graduate students.

### **Required Documents**
  * Essay
  * Reference Letters
  * Application Form

**Also Check:[Rockefeller University USA Summer Fellowship 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/rockefeller-university-usa-summer-fellowship/)**
### **How to Apply for the STScI SASP Summer Program 2026?**
Applicants must apply online from the Official website. The Link to the Official website is below. For more International Internships, we recommend that you check our **[Fully Funded Internship Category](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)**.
