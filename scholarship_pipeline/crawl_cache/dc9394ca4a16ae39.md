Are you ready to spend your summer in **Bulgaria**? The applications are now open for the Bulgaria INSAIT Summer Research Fellowship 2026. It is a**Fully Funded 8 to 12-week** summer Internship for International students from all around the world. There is no application fee. The **Objective** of the INSAIT Summer Research Fellowship is to engage students in research activities during the summer under the mentorship of **INSAIT faculty**. Bulgaria is now a Schengen country. The Summer Fellowship will be at the Institute for Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Technology (INSAIT). The Institute is Located in Sofia, Bulgaria. The student and their mentor decide the exact dates of the **Fellowship**. Below are more details on the Financial Benefits and the Application Process.
## **Details About INSAIT Summer Research Fellowship 2026 in Bulgaria**
  * **Host Country** : Bulgaria
  * **Location** : INSAIT Institute, Sofia
  * **Dates** : Decided between the Student and Mentor
  * **Duration** : 8 to 12 Weeks
  * **Financial Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 8th March 2026**

**Also Check:[ETH Robotics Summer Fellowship 2026 Switzerland (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/eth-robotics-summer-fellowship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * **Monthly Stipend:** All students will receive a 1500 EUR monthly stipend to cover living expenses.
  * **Accommodation:** INSAIT will arrange and cover the accommodation.
  * **Travel Expenses:** Travel expenses to/from Bulgaria will be covered by INSAIT.
  * **Visa Assistance:** INSAIT will assist selected candidates with visa applications.
  * Vacation during the summer Fellowship.

**Also Check:[Rotary Peace Fellowship 2027/28 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/rotary-peace-fellowship-program/)**
### **Summer Fellowship Dates**
The summer program lasts 8-12 weeks, with the exact duration determined by the student and their mentor. On-site participation in Sofia, Bulgaria, is mandatory for all students.
### **Research Areas**
Computer Vision, Robotics, Foundational Models, AI Agents, NLP, Algorithms & Theory, Quantum Computing, AI for Math, and Safe and Trustworthy AI.
**Also Check:[University of Hong Kong Summer Program 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/university-of-hong-kong-summer-program/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The INSAIT Bulgaria Summer Research Fellowship is open to all nationals.
  * Undergraduate students enrolled in a Bachelor’s degree program at any accredited institution are eligible to apply.
  * First Year Undergrad students can also apply.
  * The students should be enrolled in a STEM major, such as computer science, mathematics, physics, or engineering.
  * A minimum GPA of 3.5 (out of 4.0) is required.
  * **Note:** Graduate students or students enrolled in a Graduate degree program can apply for the **[Bulgaria INSAIT Graduate Fellowship 2026](https://opportunitiescorners.com/bulgaria-insait-graduate-fellowship/)**.

### **Required Documents**
  * CV (PDF)
  * Unofficial Transcript (PDF)
  * Motivation Letter (PDF)

**Also Check:[AYFN JAPAN Summer Culture Camp 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/ayfn-japan-summer-culture-camp/)**
### **How to Apply for the INSAIT Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship 2026 (SURF)?**
Applicants have to apply online from the official website. Submit your applications before the deadline. The Link to the official website is below.
