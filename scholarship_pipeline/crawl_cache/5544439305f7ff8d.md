Nestle is one of the largest companies that offer Thousands of internship opportunities for students. The applications are open for Nestle Internship **Globally**. All Internships are **Paid** and available in all countries where Nestle operates. The Nestle Internship Program is **open to all students around the Globe**. For candidates who are looking to complete an internship at a multinational company then Nestle is one of them to try. In Lithuania, Nestle is offering 924 EUR Gross/Month to the interns. The stipend varies by location.
**So don’t wait and start enlisting now!** Internships are open to Students, Undergraduates, and Graduates. Especially the Fresh Graduates to gain experience. An internship at Nestle will boost your skills and will have a great impact on your**CV/Resume and career.** Both males and females can apply. Almost**1,000** internships are organized by **Nestle** company available throughout the year. More details about the Nestle Internship are given below.
## **Details About Nestle Internship**
  * **Company** : Nestle
  * **Gender** : Female/Male
  * **Headquarter** : Switzerland
  * **Application process** : Online
  * **Who can Apply** : Students from all around the world

**Also Check:[Unilever Summer Internship | Early Careers at Unilever](https://opportunitiescorners.com/unilever-summer-internship-2024/)**
### **Internship Fields**
  * Human resources
  * Administration
  * Engineering
  * Communications
  * Finance
  * Marketing
  * Legal
  * Information technology (IT)
  * Internships
  * Sales
  * Research and development
  * Supply chain
  * Procurement
  * Technical
  * And production

### **Nestle Internship Benefits**
The Benefit of the Internship is that you can either work in your own country or you can go global.
All Nestle internships are paid, even in countries where this is not mandated by local laws and regulations.
### **Internship Duration**
Nestle offers internships in diverse fields. The duration of the project is **six weeks.**
### **Who can Apply for the Nestle Internship?**
  * The Internship is open to students and Graduates.
  * It is open to applicants from all around the world.
  * For most internships, nestle does not ask for IELTS or any language test.

### **What does an Internship Offer?**
At Nestle, you will be allowed to combine your technical education with applied work experience in your chosen field. You will be able to contribute and build your skills in presentation, analysis, and leadership, and meet other students.
### **Internship Location**
You can complete and apply for an internship in any **[ONE of these Locations](https://www.nestle.com/jobs)**. You can also select your present Country for the Internship.

### **Required Documents**
When applying for an Internship they might ask for the:
  1. Identity Document
  2. CV/Resume
  3. Recommendation letter
  4. Certified academic transcripts
  5. Experience letter (if you have any)
  6. Cover letter

### **How to Apply for the Nestle Internship?**
  1. **Visit the Nestle****careers** portal and fill out the form for the required field.
  2. The Website is: **<https://www.nestle.com/jobs/students-graduates>**
  3. Click on the career section.
  4. Choose from the listed areas and fields.
  5. Read Internship **[FAQs](https://www.nestle.com/jobs/faqs)**
