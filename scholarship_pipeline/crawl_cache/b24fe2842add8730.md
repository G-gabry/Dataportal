The University of Montreal (known as Universite de Montreal) is a **Public** University in Montreal, Canada. It is the **second** –**largest** university in Canada by number of students. Apply for the **University of Montreal Scholarships 2024** to study in Canada. To support talents from all around the world Université de Montréal has **established** the **UdeM exemption scholarship program intended for international students.**
They could benefit from exempting from Paying Tuition Fees. You can apply for this scholarship for the winter 2023, summer 2023, fall 2023, and winter 2024 sessions. Montreal University has a wide range of academic programs such as medicine, law, engineering, business, and the arts and sciences. More details about the Montreal University Scholarships 2024 are given below.
## **Details About University of Montreal Scholarships 2024, Canada (Funded)**
  * **Country** : Canada
  * **University Name** : Montreal University
  * **Degree Level** : Bachelors, Masters, PhD
  * **Deadline** : **[Please Check the Application Deadlines](https://admission.umontreal.ca/en/admissions/preparing-your-application/respect-official-deadlines/)**

### **Scholarship Name**
UdeM exemption scholarship program intended for international students.
### **Who can Apply for the Scholarship?**
  * Undergradaute Students
  * Master Students
  * Ph.D. Students

**Also Check:[Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship 2024/25](https://opportunitiescorners.com/stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship-2024/)**
### **Amount of the Scholarship**
**Undergraduate:**
  * Level A: $13,340.40 per year (2 sessions, equivalent to 30 credits) or $6,670.20 per session (15 credits) or $444.68 per credit
  * Level B: $6,357.60 per year (2 sessions, equivalent to 30 credits) or $3,178.80 per session (15 credits) or $211.92 per credit

**Graduate:**
  * **Non-research** : $10,474.65 per year (3 sessions, equivalent to 45 credits) or $3,491.55 per session (equivalent to 15 credits) or $232.77 per credit

This amount is applied to tuition fees of $30,829.50 per year (45 credits)*.
  * Research: $10,347.75 per year (3 sessions, equivalent to 45 credits) or $3,449.25 per session (equivalent to 15 credits) or $229.95 per credit.

**PhD** :
  * $22,241.25 per year (3 sessions, equivalent to 45 credits) or $7,413.75 per session (equivalent to 15 credits) or $494.25 per credit.

### **How You Can Apply for the University of Montreal Scholarships 2024?**
  * You have to apply for admission first. Please complete the application before the deadline.
  * **[Check the admissions requirements](https://admission.umontreal.ca/en/admissions/preparation/verify-eligibility-conditions/)** for the study programs of your interest.
  * Receive an admission offer and you will have time to accept the offer.
  * After receiving your admissions offer, the Université de Montréal will promptly inform you, by email, of the amount of the scholarship to which you are entitled.
