The applications are open for the **Kyoto University iCeMS Internship 2026** in **Japan**. The iCeMS Internship is open to **international students** from **around the world**. Kyoto University’s iCeMS invites international students currently enrolled at universities abroad to apply for its six-week **[fully funded internship](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)** program. The iCeMS Internship will run from June 30, 2026, to August 6, 2026. There is **no application fee,** and IELTS is **not required.** This internship aims to offer international students a valuable research experience and to inspire them to choose Kyoto University for their graduate studies. Participants will arrive at the Institute for Integrated Cell-Material Sciences (iCeMS), Kyoto University Institute for Advanced Study (KUIAS), Kyoto, Japan. More details are below.
## **Details About Kyoto University iCeMS Internship 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Japan
  * **Location** : Kyoto
  * **Duration** : 6 Weeks
  * **Program Dates** : June 30, 2026 – August 6, 2026
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 26th Feb 2026**

**Also Check:[Woven Toyota Japan Internship 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/woven-toyota-japan-internship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * Round-trip Economy Airfare
  * Free Accommodation
  * 50000 Japanese Yen Stipend
  * Assistance in obtaining the Visa for Travel

**Also Check:[DGIST Summer Research Internship 2026 in South Korea (Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/dgist-summer-internship-in-south-korea/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The iCeMS Internship Program is open to all Nationals.
  * Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s degree program at a foreign university.
  * Must have at least two semesters of undergraduate study remaining after program completion.
  * Demonstrated interest in pursuing graduate studies at Kyoto University within an iCeMS laboratory.
  * **Preferred Background** : Cell biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Materials Science, Chemical Biology.

**Also Check:[IMF Fund Internship Program 2026 in USA (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/imf-fund-internship/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Letter of Intent
  * CV
  * Recommendation Letter
  * University Enrollment Proof

**Also Check:[Sokendai KEK Summer Student Program 2026 in Japan (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/kek-summer-student-program/)**
### **How to Apply for the Kyoto University iCeMS Internship 2026?**
Applicants have to apply online. Participants will be selected after screening based on the application documents. The Official website is below. For more Fully Funded International Internships, kindly check out the **[Internship Category](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/internships/)**.
