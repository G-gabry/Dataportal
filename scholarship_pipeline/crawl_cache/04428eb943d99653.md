Applications are now open for the **Global Youth Masterclass Paris 2026** , a high-level forum dedicated to leadership growth, policy insight, and executive learning. Guided by a “100-Year Vision of Global Empowerment,” the event will take place in **Paris, France, from April 23 to 26, 2026**. Organized by the **Global Institute | School of Leadership and Policy** , this MasterClass is designed to unite the next generation of decision-makers and changemakers for an immersive experience in thought leadership and collaborative action. Notably, **IELTS is not required** , and a **fully funded scholarship** is available to cover all participant expenses. Further details can be found below.
## **Details About Global Youth MasterClass Paris 2026**
  * **Host Country** : France
  * **Location** : Paris
  * **Duration** : 4 Days
  * **Dates** : April 23 to 26, 2026
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 16th Feb 2026**

**Also Check:[Scout Adventures Volunteer Program 2026 UK (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/scout-adventures-volunteer-program/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Fully Funded seat covers:
  * Return Airfare
  * Meals
  * Access to Masterclass
  * Education Certification
  * Invitation Letter
  * Visa Support
  * France Visa Sponsorship

**Also Check:[InteRussia Fellowship Program 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/interussia-fellowship-program/)**
### **Financial Assistance**
The additional support for visa fees, travel expenses, and accommodation in Paris, France.
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * **Next-Generation Leaders:** Students and young individuals of all ages eager to drive future change.
  * **Executive Leadership:** Senior executives, decision-makers, and business leaders committed to social impact.
  * **Policy & Governance:** Government officials and policy professionals shaping the global landscape.
  * **Academic & Research:** Researchers, academics, educators, and international scholars.
  * **Social Impact:** NGO leaders, social entrepreneurs, and community changemakers.
  * **Young Professionals:** Emerging leaders across all sectors looking to elevate their leadership journey.

**Also Check:[Ocean Leaders Fellowship 2026 in Kenya (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/ocean-leaders-fellowship/)**
### **How to Apply for the Global Youth MasterClass Paris 2026?**
Make an online application from the official website. Pay the application fee and submit it. The link is below.
[APPLY FOR THE GLOBAL YOUTH MASTERCLASS IN PARIS](https://globalinstitute.school/programmes/)
