The **Erasmus Mundus META4.0 Scholarship** is a **Fully Funded** 2 Year **Master’s** Program in European Universities. This Program is jointly offered by **Six** Universities in Germany, Italy, Slovenia, France, and Norway. The **objective** of this Program is to fulfill the needs of society, the labor market, and manufacturing issues in the future. By attending this program, graduates will become **experts** in **Manufacturing**. This will focus on Digital Manufacturing, Clean Manufacturing, Sustainable Manufacturing, and Smart Manufacturing. The Erasmus **META4.0** program will award **90** **scholarships**. There is no application fee, and applicants from all countries are eligible to apply. This Fully Funded Master’s Scholarship in Europe will cover Tuition Fees, Visa, Airfare, Stipend, Insurance, and other Living Expenses. More details about the Erasmus Mundus META4.0 Program, its future career, the Job market in Europe, and the requirements are below.
## **Details About Erasmus Mundus META4.0 Scholarship**
  * **Host Countries** : Germany, Italy, Slovenia, France, and Norway
  * **Degree Level** : Master’s
  * **Program Name** : Erasmus Mundus META4.0 (Master’s in Manufacturing)
  * **Duration** : 2 Years
  * **Funded** **by** : European Union
  * **Deadline: 15th February 2026**

### **Financial Coverage**
The Erasmus Mundus Scholarship covers:
  * Monthly Stipend
  * Participation fees covered
  * Tuition Fees
  * Travel costs
  * Accommodation costs
  * Visa Fees
  * Other living expenses

### **Erasmus Mundus META4.0 Partner Universities**
The most prominent universities in Europe in the field of Manufacturing. The Partner universities of the Erasmus Mundus Meta4.0 Joint Master’s in Manufacturing 4.0 are:
  * Ecole Centrale de Lyon (ECL), France
  * Polytechnic University of Turin (POLITO), Italy
  * Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Norway
  * University of Ljubljana (UL), Slovenia
  * Mines Saint-Etienne (MSE), France
  * Technische Universität Chemnitz (TUC), Germany

**Also Check:[Islamic Development Bank IsDB Scholarship 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/islamic-development-bank-isdb-scholarship-2025/)**
### **Erasmus Mundus META4.0 Program Structure**
There are 4 semesters divided into:
  * **Semester 1:** Ecole Centrale de Lyon (ECL), France
  * **Semester 2:** Polytechnic University of Turin (POLITO), Italy
  * **Semester 3: Specialization with 4 Possible Tracks:**
    * Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Norway
    * University of Ljubljana (UL), Slovenia
    * Mines Saint-Etienne (MSE), France
    * Ecole Centrale de Lyon (ECL), France
    * Technische Universität Chemnitz (TUC), Germany
  * **Semester 4** : Work placement and Master’s thesis.
  *

### **Career and Future Jobs in Europe**
Why You Should Enroll in the META4.0 Program? Becoming “**Greener”** is expected to generate over 1 million jobs by 2030. 70% of companies in Europe face difficulty in finding individuals with the right skills.
**Meta4.0** has been specifically designed to address these issues. Graduates of this program will emerge as future leaders in manufacturing, equipped to work globally.

### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The Scholarship is open to all Nationals.
  * To be eligible, applicants must hold a Bachelor of Science or Engineering degree in Mechanics, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, or potentially Mechatronics.
  * Applicants in their final year of a Bachelor’s or equivalent program may apply conditionally but must graduate before July 31st, 2026.

### **IELTS/TOEFL Exemption**
Applicants who **completed their studies in English** at a **university** from one of the listed countries may apply without a language test but **must provide an official letter confirming their English proficiency.**
  * **Africa** : Botswana, Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana, Kenya, Kingdom of Eswatini, Lesotho, Malawi, Mauritius, Mozambique, Namibia, Nigeria, Rwanda, Seychelles, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Uganda, United Republic of Tanzania, Zambia
  * **Asia** : Bangladesh, Brunei Darussalam, India, Malaysia, Maldives, Pakistan, Singapore, Sri Lanka
  * **America** : USA
  * **Caribbean and Americas** : Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Canada*, Dominica, Grenada, Guyana, Jamaica, Saint Lucia, St Kitts and Nevis, St Vincent and The Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago
  * **Europe** : Cyprus, Malta, United Kingdom*, Ireland
  * **The Pacific** : Australia, Fiji, Kiribati, Nauru, New Zealand*, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Tuvalu, Vanuatu.

### **Required Documents**
  * CV in a **[Europass CV Format](https://opportunitiescorners.com/europass-cv-format/)**
  * Passport-sized photograph
  * Copy of passport showing identification details
  * Copies of degree or diploma certificates
  * Academic transcripts
  * Proof of English language proficiency
  * Evidence of the mathematics level
  * Motivation video
  * Residence certificate
  * Recommendation letters
