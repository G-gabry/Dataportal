Dear all. South Korea has become a famous hotspot for international students. From Korean Movies to Korean Noodles. And from K-Pop to Korean Higher Education. South Korea is everywhere. In this article, you will explore the **List of South Korean Fully Funded Scholarships for the Fall 2024 intake**. Apply for the **Masters** , **MPhil** , **PhD** , **MS/MPhil leading to PhD**. The scholarships for Fall 2024 intake from different Korean Universities.
Many international students frequently choose South Korea because of highly paid scholarships, easy admission process, easy visa requirements, and an affordable country to live in. South Korea boasts world-class universities with a strong focus on academics. Many universities are highly ranked internationally. I won’t take more time. Let’s come directly to the List of Fully Funded Scholarships in South Korea in 2024 for international students.
## **Professor Scholarship and University Own Scholarships in South Korea**
Apply as soon as possible for Fall 2024 Scholarships for Masters and PhD in Korean Universities, which are open now, therefore prepare to apply for them now using the information given below.
In Korea, most of the scholarships are offered by the professors. Professors hire you in the lab and you work on company projects. The professor will pay for your tuition, meals, stipend, etc.
Search relevant professors using **google.com** and search one by one with keywords such as University name + your major + Korea + professor list.
The University Scholarships are Funded by the university itself, based on merit, criteria, and requirements.
