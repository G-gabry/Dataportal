Toronto is hosting the **One Future Conference 2026**. A premier international gathering that convenes young leaders, creators, entrepreneurs, and changemakers representing over 150 nations. Applications are now open for the One Future Conference 2026, taking place in **Toronto** from **June 25 to 28, 2026**. This**4-day conference** is open to candidates from **all around the world**. Moreover, IELTS is also not required. The 2026 edition will offer **25** fully funded and **25** partially funded seats and a **grant** of **$2000** to one exceptional delegate. The world’s spotlight will shine on Toronto next summer as the city hosts the One Future Conference 2026. Featured by Ground News, National Post, Financial Post, Toronto Sun, and many other leading platforms. You may [**apply now from here**](https://one-future.org/canada2026/ref/5/).
## **Details About One Future Conference 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Canada
  * **Location** : Toronto
  * **Dates** : June 25–28, 2026
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 25th February 2026**

**Also Check:[Youth Future Summit 2026 in Geneva, Switzerland (Fully Funded)](https://thecips.org/yfs2026/ref/4/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
**The Fully Funded delegates will receive:**
  * Round-trip International Airfare
  * Accommodation in Toronto
  * Meals and coffee breaks
  * Full conference access
  * Official visa support letter
  * Conference merchandise & delegate kit
  * Opportunity to showcase your project
  * Access to networking receptions
  * Opportunity to speak in panel discussions

**The Partially Funded delegates will receive:**
  * Meals and coffee breaks
  * Full conference access
  * Official visa support letter
  * Conference merchandise & delegate kit
  * Opportunity to showcase your project
  * Access to networking receptions
  * Opportunity to speak in panel discussions

### **Skip the Selection Stress and claim your seat now! (Guaranteed Participation)**
  * **[One Future Conference Pass](https://one-future.org/canada2026/ref/5/)**
  * **[Student Pass](https://one-future.org/canada2026/ref/5/) (Discounts on Valid Student ID)**
  * **[Visa Invitation Package](https://one-future.org/canada2026/ref/5/)** (Get Invitation Letter, Documents Checklist, and Embassy coordination email)

### **Objectives of the Program**
The objective of this conference is to dive into the future of our world! Through interactive sessions and thought-provoking panels, you will explore crucial areas like **AI, Global Governance, Human Rights, Technology & Innovation, Content Creation, Cryptocurrency, FinTech, and the Future of Work**.
### **Themes of the One Future Conference**
  * The Future of Work & Leadership
  * AI, Ethics, and Global Governance
  * Cryptocurrency & Financial Innovation
  * Human Rights in the Digital Age
  * Technology, Creativity, and Content Creation
  * Climate Action, Equity & Global Inclusion.

**Also Check:[List of Conferences and Youth Summits in 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/list-of-conferences-and-youth-summits/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * **Global Participation:** Open to participants of **all nationalities**.
  * **Age Range:** Applicants must be between the ages of **17 and 50**.
  * **Diverse Backgrounds Welcomed:** Eligibility extends across a multidisciplinary spectrum, including:
    * Students and young professionals
    * Entrepreneurs and content creators
    * Innovators and human rights defenders
    * Media specialists
    * Medical, data, IT, and science professionals
    * Individuals from the humanities and social sciences
  * **Multidisciplinary Focus:** The conference is intentionally designed to bring together voices from **every field** to explore the future.
  * **Language Requirement** : No English proficiency certificate is required.

### **How to Apply for the One Future Conference 2026?**
Simply, apply online from the official website or visit: [**https://one-future.org/canada2026/ref/5/**](https://one-future.org/canada2026/ref/5/). The Link to the official website is below.
