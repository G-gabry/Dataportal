The applications are now open for the **CRG Research Training Program 2026** in **Spain**. This opportunity is open to **Bachelor** , and **Master’s** students from **all nationalities** to conduct research at the Centre for Genomic Regulation (CRG) in **Barcelona** , Spain. The program **aims** to encourage students to pursue a scientific career, giving them experience in an international laboratory while improving their skills. There is **no application fee** , and **all the expenses** are **covered** by the CRG. The **duration** of the CRG Research Training Program is **5 Months**. Below are more details about the requirements, financial benefits, and the application process.
## **Details About CRG Research Training Program 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Spain
  * **Location** : Barcelona
  * **Duration** : 5 Months
  * **Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: Open all Year**

**Also Check:[European Youth Summit 2026 in Switzerland (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/european-youth-summit-in-switzerland/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Program Covers:
  * **Stipend:** 600€/month/gross – up to 5 months
  * **Travel support:** Return ticket (up to 800€/non European flights / up to 300€ for European flights).

**Also Check:[Youth Development Forum 2026 Berlin, Germany (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/youth-development-forum-berlin/)**
### **Research Projects**
Candidates can look at the available research projects and contact the professor directly by providing them with the documents. **[Check the list here](https://www.crg.eu/en/content/research/faculty-members)**. All CRG labs are open to host Master’s and Bachelor’s students throughout the year.
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * Candidates from all countries are eligible.
  * Must be accepted by the CRG Lab.
  * Projects are available across various disciplines.

**Also Check:[Youth Leadership Certification New Zealand 2026 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/youth-leadership-certification-new-zealand/)**
### **Required Documents**
  * Motivation letter
  * Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  * Reference letter
  * University transcripts.

**Also Check:[One Future Conference 2026 in Canada (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/one-future-conference-in-canada/)**
### **How to Apply for the CRG Research Training Program 2026?**
Applications are open throughout the year. If interested, contact the project supervisor and group leader with the above-mentioned documents. The link to the official website is below.
[APPLY FOR THE CRG SPAIN TRAINING](https://www.crg.eu/en/content/training/undergraduates-and-masters)
