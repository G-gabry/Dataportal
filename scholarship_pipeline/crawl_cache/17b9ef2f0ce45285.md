The Applications are now open for the **Transilvania Academica Scholarship 2026** in **Romania**. It is a **Fully Funded[Scholarship in Europe](https://opportunitiescorners.com/category/scholarships-in-europe/)** for International students to **pursue** a **Bachelor’s,** **Master’s** , and **PhD** programs from Transilvania University. The Scholarship is open to international students from**non-EU/EEA countries.** The **Objective** of the Scholarship is to attract International students to Transilvania University to increase the Visibility among students. There is **no application fee** to apply for this scholarship. **After receiving the scholarship** , the selected candidates will apply for the Admission online for Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD programs at Transilvania University. Below are more details about the scholarship requirements, financial benefits, and the application process.
## **Details Aboit Transilvania Academica Scholarship 2026**
  * **Host Country** : Romania
  * **University Name** : Transilvania University
  * **Degree Level** : Bachelor’s, Master’s, PhD
  * **Financial Benefits** : Fully Funded
  * **Deadline: 10th April 2026**

**Also Check:[Belgium Master Mind Scholarship 2026/27 (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/belgium-master-mind-scholarship/)**
### **Financial Benefits**
The Scholarship Covers:
  * **Tuition Fees:** Covers all tuition expenses, including registration and graduation fees.
  * **Accommodation:** Provides free accommodation in university dormitories, even during holiday periods.
  * **Living Allowance:** Offers a monthly allowance of 800 lei (approximately 170 USD), which is provided throughout the year, including holidays.

### **Scholarship Duration**
  * **Undergraduate** : 3-6 Years
  * **Master** : 1.5-2 Years
  * **Doctorate** : 3-4 Years

### **Faculties**
  * Faculty of Product Design and Environment
  * Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
  * Faculty of Furniture Design and Wood Engineering
  * Faculty of Mechanical Engineering
  * Faculty of Technological Engineering and Industrial Management
  * Faculty of Silviculture and Forest Engineering
  * Faculty of Materials Science and Engineering
  * Faculty of Law
  * Faculty of Physical Education and Mountain Sports
  * Faculty of Letters
  * Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science
  * Faculty of Medicine
  * Faculty of Music
  * Faculty of Psychology and Education Sciences
  * Faculty of Sociology and Communication
  * Faculty of Economic Sciences and Business Administration

**Also Check:[YTB-IsDB Joint Scholarship 2026 in Turkey (Fully Funded)](https://opportunitiescorners.com/ytb-isdb-joint-scholarship/)**
### **Eligibility Criteria**
  * The Romanian Transilvania Academica Scholarship is open to citizens of Non-EU/EEA Countries.
  * There is no application fee.
  * Fill the online application form.
  * The selection criteria depend on the quality of the documents submitted in the application file.
  * Successful candidates will apply for the Admission later.

### **Required Documents**
The application file includes the following documents (in .pdf format):
  * Curriculum vitae
  * Motivation letter (maximum one page).
  * Three letters of recommendation.

### **Why You Should Study at the Transilvania University?**
  * This is a large, public university located in Brașov, Romania.
  * It offers a wide range of academic programs across various disciplines, including Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, Medicine, and more.
  * Renowned University, Historic/Modern City
  * Advanced Infrastructure
  * International Environment
  * Enhanced Student Life: Modern facilities and continuous improvement.
